Set up Soundboard

      

        

          
Play sounds in Discord and games. Soundboard sends audio to a virtual microphone, and other apps listen to it like a normal mic. That needs a free virtual audio cable.

          

            Checking…
            
            
          

          
Install it, restart your PC, then reopen Soundboard. It is detected automatically, so you don't need to pick anything.

          
Only want to play sounds on your own speakers? You can skip this step.

        


        

          
Test your audio. Play a short tone. You should hear it on your speakers. If the cable is connected, apps set to use CABLE Output as their microphone will hear it too.

          

            
            
          

          
Silent? Open Settings > Audio and check the Output Device.
